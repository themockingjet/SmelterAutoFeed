# SmeltAndFuel Icon Brief

This brief keeps the package icon aligned with the shared Valheim mod brand
while giving SmeltAndFuel a distinct automation silhouette.

## Brand system

- Canvas: 256x256 PNG
- Style: Nordic fantasy, low-poly, hand-painted, Valheim-adjacent
- Composition: centered object in three-quarter perspective
- Frame: shared rounded dark-metal rune frame
- Lighting: warm upper-left light with cool blue shadow
- Text: none
- Transparency: none

## Mod identity

- Mod name: SmeltAndFuel
- Category: automation
- Primary subject: a squat stone smelter with a visible hinged feed hopper
- Supporting objects: a glowing ore chunk sliding into the hopper and a split
  log entering the side fuel opening
- Accent color: ember orange with iron-blue secondary shadows
- Mood: reliable, warm, mechanical
- Subject formula: A squat stone smelter receives an ore chunk and a split log
  through its two feed openings, throwing one controlled shower of sparks, with
  a raised hinged hopper lid as the distinguishing silhouette detail.
- Avoid: Valheim logos, copied game UI, readable text, clutter,
  photorealism, extra characters, unrelated weapons, and generic gears.

## Package icon requirements

- The package icon is an original 256x256 PNG.
- The central subject is distinct from generic gear, gem, or diamond artwork.
- The standard frame, background, and lighting treatment remain consistent with
  the shared brand system.
- The final `Thunderstore/icon.png` has no text, logos, watermarks, or
  transparency.
